1. What Owtomate does

Owtomate is a job-application assistant for people looking for work in Australia. Using the résumé, details and preferences you give it, it searches SEEK and Indeed, judges which listings fit you, writes a cover letter for each, answers screening questions from your details, and submits the application through your own account on the job board or through the employer's own site. It keeps a record of everything it sends. When it cannot answer something truthfully from what you have given it, it stops and asks you.

To get applications through, it also signs your job-board accounts back in when a board ends the session, creates accounts on employer sites that require one, enters one-time codes from the mailbox you connect, and has bot checks such as CAPTCHAs solved by a third-party service. It emails you an evening summary and an alert when something stops it working for you. Section 4 and our Privacy Policy say how each of these works and which services are involved.
